# Wake Final Judge Demo Script

Target: 3:00–3:15

Opening rule: begin directly on the completed Wake hero state at Replay 8:55.
Close in Neo4j Aura Bloom.

## Before recording

- Open Wake at the completed hero state with the Work 2 video on a clear frame.
- Keep `Why Wake believes this`, `Ask Wake`, the workout, and provenance collapsed.
- Open Aura Bloom in a second tab with the Wake graph already arranged.
- In Bloom, include the `WorkoutPrescription`, five `DerivedInsight` nodes,
  `InsightCollection`, workout, segments, events, observations, providers,
  `AskWakeContext`, and supplemental Jockey branch.
- Make the `WorkoutPrescription` node easy to find.
- Do not open `Ask Wake`; its reviewed model answer is still pending.
- Do not open the in-app provenance panel unless the successful PR #1 response
  and verification artifacts have been merged.

## 0:00–0:20 — The product

**On screen:** Completed Wake Replay at 8:55.

**Say:**

“A Concept2 rower normally finishes with machine data over here and a long video
over there. Wake turns both into one reviewed Replay: the complete workout,
synchronized telemetry and footage, the moments that mattered, and what to do
next.”

**Point to:** the four work intervals, recoveries, watts, rate, heart rate,
insight markers, Goal, and Flag.

## 0:20–0:55 — A real cross-modal insight

**Action:** Click Insight 5 at 17:17.

**Say:**

“Here is the central finding. These two windows were almost identical in stroke
rate—29.8 versus 30.4 strokes per minute—but they were 58 watts apart.
Concept2 establishes that numerical difference.”

**Action:** Expand `Why Wake believes this`.

**Say:**

“Pegasus reviewed the clock-aligned frontal footage and could not reliably
explain the difference. Wake preserves that as unresolved instead of inventing
bad biomechanics. Jockey then searched independently across the video store and
found a side view with a candidate sequencing mechanism. That is useful context,
but the graph keeps it hypothesis-only: it is not attached as proof of what
happened in these workout windows.”

## 0:55–1:25 — Athlete-authored memory

**Action:** Click the Flag at 19:38, pause on its video, then click the Goal at
21:56.

**Say:**

“The athlete can also leave memory inside the workout. A finger gesture becomes
a Flag for later review, without Wake assigning it a meaning. A spoken target
becomes a Goal: keep the split under 2:15.”

**Pause on the Goal result.**

**Say:**

“Wake evaluates that note against the machine data. After the Goal, all 95
recorded samples were under target, averaging 2:03.9 per 500 meters. The Goal
preceded the result; Wake does not claim it caused it.”

## 1:25–1:50 — Multiple reviewed insights

**Action:** Sweep the cursor across the five insight markers, then select Work 4
or Insight 3.

**Say:**

“This is not a two-card summary. Aura stores five independently selectable,
deterministic findings: Work 2’s progressive build, Work 3’s late surge, Work
4’s strongest overall interval, the achieved Goal, and the similar-rate,
different-output comparison.”

## 1:50–2:20 — Turn review into the next workout

**Action:** Expand `View workout`.

**Say:**

“Wake ends with an executable next session, not a generic cue. Build pressure,
then rate is four four-minute efforts. The first two hold 28 strokes per minute
while pace improves; the final two add rate only as output rises.”

**Point to:** the ordered targets and measurable success criteria.

**Say:**

“The athlete can copy the exact ten-step variable-interval recipe into ErgData.
The success test is equally concrete: stay under 2:15, produce more power at the
same rate, avoid an interval-to-interval power drop, and finish under 2:06
without exceeding 30.5 strokes per minute.”

## 2:20–3:05 — Reveal and analyze the Aura graph

**Action:** Switch to the prepared Aura Bloom tab.

**Say:**

“This is the live Aura graph behind the Replay. Wake does not store a coaching
answer as an isolated blob. The workout connects to phases and events; events
connect to Concept2 measurements and reviewed video observations; the five
derived insights remain individually inspectable.”

**Action:** Select the `WorkoutPrescription` node and expand or highlight its
neighbors.

**Say:**

“Here is the analysis that matters: the next workout is supported by all five
reviewed insights. Follow another branch and you reach the exact performance
window, provider evidence, counterevidence, and camera limitation that earned
each claim.”

**Action:** Select the `AskWakeContext` or supplemental Jockey branch.

**Say:**

“The graph also enforces uncertainty. Jockey’s side-view finding stays
supplemental and clip-local, while Pegasus’s unresolved result remains attached
to the calibrated comparison. Aura makes those evidence boundaries visible,
queryable, and reusable.”

## 3:05–3:15 — Close

**Hold on:** the Bloom scene with the selected prescription and its five insight
relationships visible.

**Say:**

“That is Wake: one workout becomes a connected memory—what happened, what the
athlete noticed, what the evidence supports, and the exact workout to do next.”
